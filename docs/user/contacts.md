# Contacts — quick guide

**Contacts** holds every company and person you work with. Every other app (CRM, Sales, Calendar,
Discuss) uses the same address book.

- **Companies and people.** Tick *Company* for an organisation. People belong to a company through the
  *Company* field; addresses (invoice, delivery) are extra contacts of the company with a type.
  A new person inherits the company's address.
- **Tags, industry, salesperson** help you filter and group (search bar → *Group by*).
- **Discussion.** The panel on the right logs changes, emails and notes, and lets you plan activities.
- **Duplicates.** Select two or more contacts in the list and choose **Merge**. Pick the contact to keep
  and, for each field that differs, which value wins. Everything that referred to the merged contacts —
  leads, quotations, meetings, messages, attachments — moves to the kept one; the others are archived.
  You can **undo** a merge for 7 days (Contacts → Configuration → Merge history, or the Undo button
  right after merging).
- **Customer portal.** On a contact with an email address, **Grant portal access** sends an invitation
  to set a password. **Revoke portal access** signs them out and disables the account.
