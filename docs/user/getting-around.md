# Getting around Tessera

Every app works the same way: learn one and you know them all.

## The suite bar

The dark bar across the top of every page:

| Element | What it does |
|---|---|
| ▦ App launcher | opens the list of apps you can use; pick one to open its first page |
| Tessera | goes to Home |
| App name | the app you are in |
| Search | opens the command palette: type the name of a menu (“invoices”, “pipeline”) or of a record and press Enter. Shortcut: <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> on a Mac) |
| 🔔 Notifications | what was assigned to you or needs your attention; the number is how many are unread |
| Company | when you work for several companies, choose which ones you see and which one new records belong to |
| Your initials | dark mode on or off, and sign out |

## Home

One tile per app you can use, with the app's first sections. Your role decides which apps appear.

## The app navigation

The panel on the left lists the app's pages, grouped by section (for Accounting: Customers, Vendors, Bank,
Reporting…). The page you are on is highlighted. **«** narrows the panel to icons and **»** widens it again;
your browser remembers the choice. On a phone the panel opens from **☰**.

## List pages

- **Title row**: the page name and the view switcher (List, Kanban, Calendar, Pivot, Graph — the views the
  page offers).
- **Command bar**: **New** (<kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>N</kbd>), **Import**, **Export**, then the search box
  (<kbd>/</kbd> to focus it), **Filters** and, where available, **✦ Ask AI**, which turns a question into
  filters you can check before applying.
- **Grid**: click a column title to sort, tick rows for bulk actions; status pills are green when done or paid,
  blue in progress, amber when waiting, red when cancelled or unpaid. Totals at the bottom add up amounts
  only (and the columns a page asks to total).
- **Kanban**: cards show the amount, the priority as stars, the owner's initials and the tags; drag a card to
  another column to move it.

## Record pages

- **Command bar** at the top: **Back**, **Save** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>S</kbd>), **Discard**
  (<kbd>Esc</kbd>), the record's own actions (Confirm, Register payment, Send by email…), **Print** and
  **Delete**.
- **Header**: the kind of record (Customer invoice, Opportunity, Quotation), its name, and its key facts:
  amount, owner and status. A dot after the name means unsaved changes.
- **Stage bar**: where the record is in its process. Done steps are ticked; click a step to move the record
  when you are allowed to.
- **Details** in one card, then the tabs (lines, notes, other information).
- **Right-hand panel**: the AI assistants of the record, marked ✦ AI, then messages, notes and activities.
  AI suggestions are proposals: nothing changes until you accept them.

## Themes and languages

Tessera follows your device's light or dark setting; **your initials → Toggle dark mode** overrides it for
this browser. The language is the one set on your user (English and French are complete).

## When a page is not available

If a link leads to a page your role does not include, Tessera says so and offers a link back to Home. Ask an
administrator if you need access.
