# Customer portal — quick guide

Customers with portal access sign in with their email address and see:

- **My documents** — the quotations sent to their company, with status, totals and PDF;
- **a quotation page** to review lines, download the PDF, accept with their typed name or decline;
- **My details** — to update their phone and address.

They never see internal notes, other customers' documents or anything else in your back office.
Invite a customer from their contact (*Grant portal access*); the invitation link works once and
expires after 7 days. Links in quotation emails work without signing in.
