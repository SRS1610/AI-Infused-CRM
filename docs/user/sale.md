# Quotations — quick guide

- **Create** a quotation from Sales → Quotations or from an opportunity (*New quotation*). Add product
  lines (price, tax and unit come from the product), free text lines, *sections* and *notes* to
  structure it, and *optional* lines the customer may add later (not in the totals).
- **Send by email.** Attaches the PDF and a personal link where the customer can view, download,
  accept (by typing their name) or decline — no account needed. The quotation moves to *Sent*.
- **Validity.** After the *Valid until* date, sent quotations expire automatically.
- **States.** Draft → Sent → Accepted / Declined / Expired; *Cancel* and *Back to draft* when needed.
  States change only through these buttons, so the history stays reliable.
- **Print** from the form (*Print → Quotation*) using your company's layout.
